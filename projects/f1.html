Inside the project

Choose a year, race, session, driver, and lap. Compare speed, throttle, braking, and sectors; replay the circuit or export a CSV.

OpenF1 telemetry covers available sessions from 2023 onward. Race results go back to 1950 through F1DB. A bundled Monza example opens instantly.

Traces use estimated distance. Some recordings have gaps; live sessions are not supported.
