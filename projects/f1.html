The race beneath
the race.

A lap time tells you the result. Lap Lab helps you investigate how it happened. Compare Norris, Piastri, Leclerc, and Sainz using recorded Monza qualifying telemetry from OpenF1.

The app runs entirely in your browser. Its bundled historical dataset avoids API keys, server cold starts, and external requests during a demo. React and TypeScript coordinate the SVG charts, distance slider, and circuit replay.

Distance is estimated from sampled speed and normalized to circuit length. Official lap and sector times are shown separately; the overlays are approximate, and recorded laps may include deleted times.

Built with: React, TypeScript, SVG, Python data preparation, OpenF1.
