Photos Classifier.

A facial recognition crawler that processes and sorts 50GB+ of competition photos using 128-dimensional facial encodings.

View source ↗

Behind the build.

After a full season of FIRST Robotics competitions, our team had amassed over 50GB of raw, unsorted event photography. Finding individual photos of specific drive team members and pit crew manually was nearly impossible. I built an automated image crawler to solve the problem with computer vision.

  • Profile Learning: Parses a small directory of reference portraits to extract 128-dimensional facial embedding vectors using dlib’s deep residual network.
  • Bulk Batch Crawling: Streams through 50GB+ of high-resolution images in-place without duplicating large directories until positive matches are found.
  • Euclidean Distance Matching: Compares detected faces against known profiles with strict tolerance thresholds to eliminate false positives in crowded competition arenas.
  • Autonomous File Organization: Automatically categorizes and routes matching images into clean, per-person folders.

Built with: Python, OpenCV, face_recognition (dlib), NumPy
