Behind the build.

This project began with a practical problem: finding people in a large collection of robotics event photographs. The rebuilt desktop app turns that unfinished script into a local scan, review, and export workflow.

  • Local computer vision: OpenCV YuNet finds faces and SFace compares them with portraits you organize into one folder per person.
  • Resumable scans: A SQLite catalog remembers unchanged files, rescans changed files, and records corrupt-image errors.
  • Review first: Inspect highlighted faces, compare similarity scores, and approve or reject candidates. Close matches between profiles are flagged as ambiguous.
  • Safe export: Copy approved photos into person folders while preserving nested filenames. Original photos are never moved or overwritten.

Models run on your computer. No photo uploads, cloud account, or subscription. Face matches can be wrong and require review; large-library accuracy depends on portrait quality and the photos being scanned.

Built with: Python, OpenCV YuNet/SFace, SQLite, Pillow, Tkinter.
