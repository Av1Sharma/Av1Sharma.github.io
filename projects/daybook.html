Daybook

A local task notebook for the browser and Mac.

Open Daybook · Download for Mac · Watch demo · View source

  • Organize tasks with due dates, categories, and daily or weekly repeats.
  • Keep completion history and review your week.
  • Store personal notes and check-ins; export JSON backups.
  • No account required. Data stays on your device, with no automatic cloud sync.
