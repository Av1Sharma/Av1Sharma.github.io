Betterness.

A SwiftUI wellness app that tracks moods, journals, and personal growth metrics.

View source ↗

Behind the build.

Betterness is a native iOS self-improvement and mindfulness application designed with Apple's human interface guidelines. It helps users build consistent personal habits, track emotional well-being, and write structured daily journals with complete on-device privacy.

  • Habit Streak Tracking: Daily habit checklist with progress rings and streak counters.
  • Mood Telemetry: Fast emotional check-ins to monitor mood patterns over weekly and monthly intervals.
  • Private Journaling: Distraction-free daily reflection notes stored securely on-device.
  • Fluid Native Experience: Built 100% with Swift and SwiftUI for smooth 120Hz ProMotion responsiveness.

Built with: Swift, SwiftUI, Combine
